A tile's size is now columns x rows, like `"2x1"`, `"3x1"` for a wide strip or `"1x2"` for a tall one. The old names `s`, `m`, `l` and `xl` still work. Long lists scroll: press ↓ on the last row in view, or use the trackpad or mouse wheel over the tile, and a small "↓ 5" shows what's left below.
