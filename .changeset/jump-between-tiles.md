Jump between tiles from the keyboard: Tab and ⇧Tab go to the next and previous tile, and ⌥ with an arrow to the next tile that way, wherever you are in its list. You land on the row you were last on in that tile. `"jumpKey"` picks another key than ⌥, or `"none"`, and Settings → General → Glance sets it too. The bar under the tiles shows the keys.
